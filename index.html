CT600 summary

This is a preparation aid, not tax advice. Rates, limits and rules come from the editable rate pack, which you must keep current. The tool does not cover tonnage tax, insurance, oil and gas, REITs, charities, hybrid mismatch rules, short-life assets, the intangibles regime in detail, land remediation, creative reliefs or Pillar Two top-up calculations; enter their effect through manual adjustments and credits. A qualified person must review every computation before it is filed.
